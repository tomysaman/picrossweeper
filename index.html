Picrossweeper a picross × minesweeper puzzle

Each number tells you how many light tiles sit in the 3×3 block around it (the numbered tile counts itself). Every tile — numbered or not — is either light or dark. Fill the whole grid to reveal the picture.
Left‑click / drag: light Right‑click / drag: dark Click again: clear
Numbers fade once their 3×3 block is fully satisfied. Every puzzle is generated to be solvable by pure logic — no guessing needed.
Keys: N new puzzle · Ctrl+Z undo · X tap colour · M music. Music starts on your first click on the board.
