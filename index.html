One giant picture, hundreds of regions. The whole painting is one grid, cut into 15×15 regions (outlined). Each number says how many light tiles are in the 3×3 block around it — the numbered tile counts itself, and only tiles inside the same region count. Mark every tile light or dark; when a region is fully correct it reveals the painting. Every region is solvable by pure logic.
Left‑click / drag: light   Right‑click / drag: dark   click again: clear
Move around: mouse wheel zooms · middle‑drag, Space+drag or the ✋ tool pans · arrows pan · F fits · pinch on touch · click the minimap to jump.
Keys: Ctrl+Z undo · X tap colour · M music · ? this panel.
